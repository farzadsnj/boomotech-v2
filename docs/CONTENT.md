# Content Strategy and Model

## Content goals

Content must help a visitor diagnose their situation, trust BoomoTech, understand the next step, and take action. Write for customers first and search engines second.

## Homepage working copy framework

### Hero

Headline direction: “Practical technology support and smarter systems for your business.”

Supporting idea: Brisbane-based assistance for day-to-day IT problems, cloud, security, websites, automation and AI—with remote options across Australia.

Primary CTA: “Get IT help”  
Secondary CTA: “Book a consultation”

Treat this as working copy pending owner approval.

### Problem-based choices

- I need help with an IT problem
- I want to improve or secure my business systems
- I need a website, app, or automation
- I want advice before buying technology

## Service content template

Each service record should include:

- title and slug
- short promise
- target customer
- problems/signals
- outcomes
- inclusions
- exclusions
- delivery modes
- process
- prerequisites
- pricing approach or “quote required”
- expected timing only when approved
- FAQ
- related services
- related articles
- primary CTA
- SEO title and description
- structured-data eligibility
- review/publish dates and author

## Article model

- title, slug and summary
- content type: article, guide, checklist, comparison, FAQ
- audience and intent
- author/reviewer
- published and updated dates
- factual sources where appropriate
- cover image and alt text
- local branded hero image, meaningful alt text and optional visual theme/focal treatment
- body with table of contents for long pieces
- related service and next action
- tags/categories
- SEO fields

## Editorial rules

- Use Australian spelling.
- Prefer short sentences and concrete examples.
- Explain acronyms at first use.
- Never publish generated statistics without a reputable source.
- Time-sensitive technical, price, legal, compliance, or security claims require source and review date.
- Security content must not encourage unsafe access or credential sharing.
- Clearly label affiliate relationships, sponsored content, and product conflicts.
- Keep a visible “last reviewed” date on guidance that can age.

## Conversion without pressure

Each page should offer one context-matched next action. Educational content can point to a relevant service, checklist, or consultation, but should remain genuinely useful without purchase.

## Content required from owner

- Approved company story and founder/team information
- Verified experience and credentials
- Service area and hours
- Service catalogue and delivery boundaries
- Prices or pricing approach
- Contact channels
- Real project examples and permission to identify clients
- Product catalogue, suppliers, inventory and fulfilment model
- Existing logo/brand assets
- Final policy text

## Current practical resource model

The resources hub groups real routes by customer need. Each card records a topic, format, approximate reading time, related service and clear action. A resource must resolve to an existing reviewed article, service, support guide, booking preparation page or the explicitly labelled sample catalogue; unfinished downloads are not presented as available.

Verified founder copy may state that Farzad Sanjarani is a Brisbane-based IT professional with a Master of Information Technology in Software Development from QUT and practical experience across IT support, endpoint deployment, networking, Microsoft 365, cloud, websites, software and automation. Employer names, client names, tenure, awards and unverified credentials remain excluded.
